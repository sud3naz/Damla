How it works

1

Sign once. You pre-sign one transaction per purchase — amount, asset and destination are locked inside each signature.

2

The protocol keeps time. Each transaction is invalid until its date arrives (Stellar's minSeqAge). Nobody can run it early — not even us.

3

XLM lands in your wallet. On every scheduled date a path payment swaps your USDC to XLM on Stellar's DEX. Miss one? The chain continues.
