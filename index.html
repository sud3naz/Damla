How it works

1

Sign once. You pre-sign one transaction per week — amount, asset and destination are locked inside each signature.

2

The protocol keeps time. Each transaction is invalid until its week arrives (Stellar's minSeqAge). Nobody can run it early — not even us.

3

XLM lands in your wallet. Every week a path payment swaps your USDC to XLM on Stellar's DEX. Miss a week? The chain continues.
