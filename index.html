Buy XLM on your schedule.
Sign only once.

Non-custodial recurring purchases on Stellar, daily, weekly or monthly. Your funds stay in your wallet; the protocol enforces the schedule. Drop by drop.

  • Non-custodial. USDC never leaves your wallet until each swap settles.
  • No smart contract. Nothing to deploy, nothing to audit, nothing to exploit.
  • Protocol-enforced timing. Purchases can't run early: Stellar validators reject them.
  • Not even we can change it. Amount, asset, floor and dates are inside your signature.
  • Cancel anytime. Move your USDC and the plan simply stops.

How it works

1

Sign in one sitting. You pre-sign one transaction per purchase. Amount, asset, destination, floor and time window are locked inside each signature.

2

The protocol keeps time. Each transaction is invalid outside its window (time bounds) and until a full period has passed since the previous one (minSeqAge). Nobody can run it early, not even us.

3

XLM lands in your wallet. On each date a path payment swaps your USDC to XLM on Stellar's DEX. Miss one? minSeqNum keeps the rest of the chain valid.
