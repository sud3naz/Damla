Damla turns your USDC into XLM every day, week or month, straight from your own wallet. No deposit, no smart contract, nothing to trust but the Stellar network.

Start a plan How it works

Works with Freighter. Free, open source, currently on Stellar testnet.

Three steps, one sitting

You decide the amount and the rhythm. The protocol keeps the calendar.

1

Choose your plan

For example: 25 USDC worth of XLM every week, 12 times. Connect Freighter and sign each purchase once. Amount, dates and price floor are locked inside your signature.

2

Stellar keeps time

Every purchase is invalid until its date. Not by our promise, by consensus: validators reject it early. Nobody can move it up, change it, or touch anything else in your wallet.

3

XLM lands in your wallet

On each date the swap runs on Stellar's own exchange. USDC leaves your wallet only at that moment, XLM arrives in the same wallet. Miss a week? The plan continues.

Why there is no contract

Most "automation" asks you to hand over funds or permissions. Damla asks for neither.

  • Your USDC stays yours. There is no vault. Nothing is deposited, so there is nothing to withdraw or lose.
  • Nothing to audit, nothing to exploit. The rules are Stellar's own transaction rules, not code we wrote.
  • Even we cannot change it. A hacked service can at most fail to submit. Then nothing happens.
  • Cancel by moving your USDC. Or press stop on your plan page. No lock-up, no penalty.

Questions

Do you hold my money?

No. Your USDC sits in your own wallet until each scheduled swap settles. The XLM you buy lands in the same wallet.

What if the price spikes on a purchase day?

You pick a ceiling when you sign (up to +10%, +25% or +50% above today's price). Above it, the purchase waits and is skipped if the price does not come back. A falling price never blocks a purchase.

What does it cost?

Nothing. Stellar fees are fractions of a cent and the service pays them. The swap runs at market price, never below your floor.

What if Damla disappears?

Export your signed plan from its page. Anyone can submit those transactions inside their windows, nobody can alter them. Your plan does not depend on us.
