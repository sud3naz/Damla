FAQ

Do you hold my money?

No. Your USDC sits in your own wallet until the moment each scheduled swap settles on Stellar's DEX. There is no deposit, no vault, no contract balance. The XLM you buy lands directly in the same wallet.

What exactly am I signing?

One Freighter transaction adds the exact hashes of your future purchases as pre-authorized signers on your Stellar account. Each hash binds a single swap: a fixed USDC amount to XLM, your destination, a minimum-received floor, and a time window. Freighter shows the signer hashes in the setup transaction; it does not display every future swap in that one prompt. Before that prompt, the site decodes every transaction, checks the hashes and shows the verified terms for your confirmation. The channel account pays the fees for later purchases.

How many times do I approve in Freighter?

Once for the plan setup. Stellar then recognizes only the exact future transaction hashes you approved. A single-approval plan supports 2 to 18 purchases because Stellar limits the number of additional account signers. The setup temporarily reserves 0.5 XLM for each purchase and one cleanup transaction; this reserve returns as the signers are consumed or removed.

Can Damla run my purchases early, or drain my account?

After setup, no. Each transaction is invalid until its scheduled window opens, and until a full period has passed since the previous purchase. Stellar validators enforce that. Your setup signature installs only the exact future transaction hashes, so changing an amount, destination or floor invalidates the authorization. A broken service can withhold a purchase, but it cannot create a different authorized payment. At setup, the independent browser check matters because Freighter shows hashes rather than the full future swaps.

What is the channel account?

A brand-new Stellar account created for your plan alone. It is the source of every transaction, pays the fees from its own reserve and owns the sequence clock that the protocol uses to keep time. The service clears its key from the active database when your plan is activated. Historical database pages or backups may retain older copies; these files must stay private.

How do I cancel?

The plan page's Stop button tells the service to stop and submit the pre-authorized cleanup, which removes unused authorizations and releases their XLM reserve. If the service is unavailable, the plan export includes the cleanup envelope you can submit; you can also remove signers manually in Freighter or Stellar Lab. Anyone who has already exported a purchase XDR can still submit it until its window closes unless its signer is removed.

What if XLM spikes on one of my purchase dates?

You chose a ceiling when you signed (+10%, +25% or +50% above the price at signing). If XLM is above it when the window opens, the purchase waits; if the price does not come back within the window, it is skipped and the plan continues with the next one. A falling price never blocks a purchase, you just receive more XLM.

What happens if a purchase is missed?

The chain is built to survive gaps: each purchase is valid across a range of sequence numbers, so a skipped one does not break the ones after it. Each window is two periods long, so a late submission is not lost either.

What are the fees?

Stellar network fees are fractions of a cent and are paid by the plan's channel account, not you. If the network is congested and the minimum fee rises above what was signed, the service wraps your unchanged transaction in a fee bump paid by its treasury (a Stellar protocol feature, no new signature from you, nothing in the transaction changes). The swap itself executes at DEX market price, never below your floor. Damla takes nothing.

Which wallets are supported?

Freighter, with a regular Stellar account (G-address). No special account type or contract deployment is needed.

Is there a smart contract? Has it been audited?

There is no smart contract, which is the point: there is no contract code to audit or exploit. The schedule and the limits are enforced by Stellar protocol rules (time bounds, minSeqAge, minSeqNum, signed transaction hashes). The testnet proof in the repository shows each rule being enforced by the network.

What if Damla shuts down?

Export your plan from the plan page. You get every channel-signed purchase envelope and the pre-authorized cleanup envelope. Anyone can submit a purchase inside its window and nobody can alter it. If the service disappears, you can submit the purchases or the cleanup yourself to release unused signer reserve.

Is this on mainnet?

Damla runs on testnet today. Everything works the same on mainnet; it needs a funded treasury for channel reserves, which is the next step. The network switch at the top right shows what this server has enabled.
